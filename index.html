Before you ask

Straight answers

Which codes and standards do you work to?
Piping: ASME B31.3, ASME B31.1, AS 4041, EN 13480. Structural: AISC 360 and AS/NZS 1170. Vessels: ASME Sec VIII. Concrete: AS 3600 and ACI 318. Tell us the code on your project and we confirm it before quoting.
How quickly will I get a quote?
We reply within 24 hours. The time needed for the work itself depends on scope, and we confirm a delivery date in the quote.
Is my project data confidential?
NDA on request. Uploaded drawings, models and messages are handled under the agreed NDA.
How do time zones work with an India delivery centre?
Our sales contacts sit in Sydney and London, and engineering is delivered from India. The clocks in the contact section show each city live, so you can see when your day overlaps with ours.
How many revisions are included?
We state the revision policy in each quote, so it matches the size and type of the job.
What do you need to quote?
The drawings or specification, the scope you want covered, the code to follow, and your deadline. A link to a shared folder is enough.
